About the studio

A neighbour
with a lab

HypercubedLabs was born from a simple idea — what if a local café could have the same creative tools as a big agency? Turns out, they can.

Based in Barcelona, we work mostly with the people and businesses nearby. We combine 3D printing, resin craft, web development, and visual design under one roof.
